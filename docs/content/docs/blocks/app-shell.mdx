Every level-2 composite in one screen, arranged the way a real product arranges them: an
`InstanceSwitcher` in the sidebar header, `SidebarNav` in its content, `SidebarUser` in its
footer, and a `SidebarInset` holding breadcrumbs above a `Section` of `StatCard`s and a
`DataTable`.

It exists because the pieces only prove themselves together. A `StatCard` looks fine in a
384px box; whether four of them sit correctly under a page header, next to a sidebar that
collapses to icons, is a different question — and the one a consumer actually has.

Worth exercising in the frame: collapse the sidebar with <kbd>⌘</kbd>+<kbd>B</kbd> and watch
the switcher and user rows fall back to bare avatars, sort and filter the table, and open the
account menu to see log out as an ordinary destructive `menuItems` entry rather than a
built-in auth flow.

## Source

```text
docs/blocks/app-shell/
├── default.tsx   — the screen
└── data.tsx      — one fake tenant's instances, nav and connections
```

Imports come from two entry points: `@kanzo-tech/ui` for the shell and composites, and
`@kanzo-tech/ui/table` for `DataTable` — a subpath, so the base bundle never carries TanStack
Table for consumers who do not use it.
