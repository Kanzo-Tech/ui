## Usage

**There is no floating panel to import.** A surface that floats over a canvas, a map or a graph, with
an edge to drag, is a composition: a positioned `div` with `Popover`'s surface, and a pointer handler
on a `separator`. The example above is the whole of it — copy it into your product and change what
your canvas needs.

It used to ship as `FloatingPanel` and `FloatingPanelResizeHandle`. No product called it, and the
name was Ark's for a different thing: Ark's floating-panel is a machine with a drag handle, a stage,
minimise and maximise, which Shark wraps under the same name. A component with no call site is an
example ([Admission](/docs/design/admission#a-composite-stays-where-a-product-calls-it)), and the
name stays free for the machine the day something needs it.

## What it does

- **The caller owns where it floats.** It sets no position of its own; place it with `className` —
  `absolute inset-y-2 end-2` over a relative parent.
- **The surface is `Popover`'s**, at its radius and shadow, translucent and blurred because the
  reader is still looking at what is underneath.
- **The drag edge is a `separator`**, `aria-orientation="vertical"`, on the panel's start, and the
  width is clamped between a minimum and a maximum. Move it to the end by putting it last and
  growing the panel as the pointer moves towards the end.
