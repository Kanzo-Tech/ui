title: The shell's dock
description: Why a page switches its side panels with one part, an activity bar of icons named by their tooltips — and what would reverse it.
---

## One dock switcher, an activity bar

**A page whose side panel holds one of several tools switches them with `ShellDockSwitcher`: an
icon per panel, named by its tooltip and its accessible name, checked while its panel is open, and
pressed again to collapse it.** It is a single-select `ToggleGroup`, so Ark makes the switcher a
`radiogroup` and each icon a `radio` with `aria-checked` — not a toggle button with `aria-pressed`. The panel's own title says what it is in words. The reference is
VS Code's activity bar. Keasy's Discover (Info · Ask · Rules · Settings) and its studio (Sources)
draw the same part.

```tsx
<ShellDockSwitcher aria-label="Side panels" value={panel} onValueChange={setPanel}>
  <ShellDockItem value="info" label="Info" icon={InfoIcon} />
  <ShellDockItem value="ask" label="Ask" icon={MessageCircleIcon} />
</ShellDockSwitcher>
```

**It replaced two ways of doing one thing**: Discover's `ToggleGroup` of bare icons in its footer,
and the studio's `Toggle` with an icon and the word *Sources* in a status bar of its own. A reader
learnt each, and a test found each differently.

**What would reverse it:** a dock with panels a reader cannot tell apart by icon, as tested with
readers. Then the item draws its label beside the icon everywhere, not in one page.

*Held by* `packages/ui/src/layouts/shell.test.tsx`, "an item is named by its label, pressed while
open, and collapses its panel when pressed again".

## The page names the switcher, and its panels keep their type

**`ShellDockSwitcher` requires an accessible name and is generic over the page's panel id.** It is a
`radiogroup`, whose name WAI-ARIA 1.2 requires, and the name it used to default to, *Panels*, misnamed
the studio's dock of one, *Sources*: only the page knows what its panels are, so the type asks for
`aria-label` or `aria-labelledby`, as `ButtonGroup`'s does. `value` and `onValueChange` are typed by
the page's id, inferred from `value`, because a page that keeps its panel in a union had to narrow a
`string` back to it — keasy's Discover with a `find` over its panels. The items stay `string`: a
child cannot carry a type to its parent in JSX, so typing them would check nothing.

**What would reverse it:** a name derivable from what the switcher holds that readers hear as right on
a dock of one and of many — then it returns as a default. A way for the items to type the group
would let the id be inferred from them instead.

*Held by* `packages/ui/src/layouts/shell.test.tsx`, "is a radiogroup named by the page, and hands back
the page's own panel type".
