The other shape a shell takes: no page, no scroll, a canvas that fills whatever is left — here a
knowledge-graph explorer modelled on keasy's discovery screen. `ShellMain` holds the canvas
full-bleed; a floating glass inspector overlays its trailing edge with Info / Ask / Rules /
Settings tabs (drag its left edge to resize); a collapsible distributions strip runs along the
bottom; and a toggle floats clear of the panel.

The graph itself is a placeholder — the design system ships no graph engine, and that boundary is
the point of a showcase. Everything around it is library parts — `Tabs`, `Slider`, `Switch`,
`ScrollArea`, `Badge`, `Button` — composed inside the `Shell` regions.

Worth exercising in the frame: switch inspector tabs, drag the panel's leading edge to resize it,
toggle it shut, collapse the distributions strip, and move the Settings sliders.

## Source

```text
docs/showcases/workspace/
```

The mini distribution charts and the faux graph are hand-rolled here rather than pulled from the
library — a showcase is allowed a product-specific part, and neither is a design-system component
(yet).
