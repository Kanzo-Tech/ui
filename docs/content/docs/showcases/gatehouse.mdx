The guild's gatehouse: who may pass, and as which hall. It opens **signed out**, so the first
thing in the frame is the screen [`@kanzo-tech/auth`](/docs/auth) ships none of — and the second,
after the button, is the shell that screen leads to.

<Callout type="warn">
**What the client knows about its roles is for drawing, never for deciding** — the rule is
[on the front page of the package](/docs/auth#the-one-rule-to-read-before-anything-else), and this
showcase says it on screen as well, because a reader who copies the arrangement without the sentence
has copied a vulnerability.
</Callout>

## What it draws

- **A sign-in screen**, and there is not one auth component in it: a `Card`, a `Button`, two
  `Link`s and a square brand tile. That is [the absence](/docs/auth#what-it-is-not) drawn: ordinary
  parts, arranged around a logo, a sentence and a legal line that no two products answer alike.
- **A hall switcher driven by `useOrganization`.** It lists all five halls, including the two the
  session carries no charter in, because that is the case worth seeing: addressing a hall you do not
  belong to resolves to `isMember: false` and an explanation, **never silently to the first hall you
  do belong to**. The alias is held by the screen here and read off the URL in a product —
  [which is why the session does not carry it](/docs/auth/session#there-is-no-active-organization-on-it).
- **A real log out.** It is an ordinary `MenuItem` with `variant="destructive"` in the sidebar
  identity row, and the `AlertDialog` beside it — its wording, and the fact that there is a
  confirmation at all — belongs to the product. This is the destination for the callout on
  [Sidebar](/docs/navigation/sidebar): the library has no logout affordance, so here is what one
  looks like when you write it.
- **Role-gated UI.** Ravenna is a warden in the Amber Hall and an archivist or a scout in the two
  she visits. `<Gate role="warden" organization={alias}>` wraps the **Charter** nav row and the
  **Post a contract** button, so the same person — one unchanged session — loses both by switching
  halls. A gated row is simply absent: no disabled control, no explanation of what you are missing.

Worth trying in the frame: sign in, then switch to **Ash Co.** or **Lanternwood** from the rail's
switcher to meet the not-a-member state, switch to **Salt** or **The Nine** to watch the warden's
two controls disappear, and open the user menu to log out and start again.

## Running it without an identity provider

`Auth` is five members — `getSession`, `subscribe`, `signIn`, `signOut` and a `fetch` — which is
small enough to fake, and that smallness is the same property that lets one provider serve a
backend-for-frontend and a browser client without anything below it knowing which is underneath.

The fake lives in `@/lib/guild-auth`, shared with the [auth pages](/docs/auth), and its own header
says why it builds a claim set rather than a `Session`. The showcase's `data.tsx` adds one thing —
it starts the session `null`, outside the gate — and `signIn` / `signOut` flip that local state so
the whole flow is live.

## Source

```text
docs/showcases/gatehouse/
├── default.tsx   — the sign-in card, the shell, the hall switcher, the user menu and its
│                   confirmation, and the two Gates
└── data.tsx      — the fake Auth, signed out to begin with; the five halls with their charters,
                    the nav column and each hall's board
```

One entry point for the parts and one for the session: `@kanzo-tech/ui` and `@kanzo-tech/auth`.
The second is a sibling package rather than a subpath, because the
[first admission rule](/docs/philosophy#admission) excludes auth from the library by name.
