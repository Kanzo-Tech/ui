## Usage

```tsx
import {
  FindingsActions,
  FindingsBadge,
  FindingsContent,
  FindingsGroup,
  FindingsHeader,
  FindingsItem,
  FindingsList,
  FindingsRoot,
  FindingsSource,
  groupFindings,
  PopoverDescription,
  tallyFindings,
} from "@kanzo-tech/ui";
```

```tsx
const groups = groupFindings(findings, (f) => `${f.rule.id}|${f.place.path}`);
const tally = tallyFindings(groups);

<FindingsRoot tally={tally}>
  <FindingsBadge>{(t) => (t ? `${t.violation} violations · ${t.warning} warnings` : "No rules")}</FindingsBadge>
  <FindingsContent>
    <FindingsHeader>
      <FindingsSource>flights-shapes.ttl · 4 rules</FindingsSource>
      <FindingsActions>
        <Button aria-label="Check again" size="icon-sm" variant="ghost"><RotateCwIcon /></Button>
      </FindingsActions>
      <PopoverDescription>1,204 of 3,310 nodes checked · 1,199 conform</PopoverDescription>
    </FindingsHeader>
    <FindingsList empty={<ChooseRules />}>
      <FindingsGroup title="ProjectShape" tally={tally} action={{ label: "Show 44", run: showAll }}>
        {groups.map((group) => (
          <FindingsItem
            key={group.rule.id}
            finding={group}
            describe={describe}
            action={{ label: `Show ${group.count}`, run: () => show(group.places) }}
          >
            {group.places[0].path}
          </FindingsItem>
        ))}
      </FindingsGroup>
    </FindingsList>
  </FindingsContent>
</FindingsRoot>
```

A SHACL report and a compiler's diagnostics are the same list: **one result, or many that share a
rule, each pointing at a place.** The place is yours — a focus node and a path, a line of a program —
and the library reaches it only through `describe`. Why it is shaped so is on
[the design page](/docs/design/findings).

## The model

```ts
type FindingSeverity = "violation" | "warning" | "info";

interface Finding<Place> {
  severity: FindingSeverity;
  message: string;
  rule: { id: string; label: string };
  place: Place;
  help?: string;
}

interface FindingGroup<Place> {
  rule: FindingRule;
  severity: FindingSeverity;
  message: string;
  count: number;
  places: Place[];
  sample: Finding<Place>[];
}
```

A `Finding` is **always one result**. A `FindingGroup` is many: its `count`, every place, and a
`sample` of the first few to open. `groupFindings(findings, keyOf, sample = 3)` folds findings by
your key — usually the rule and the path — worst severity first and in your order within one.
`tallyFindings(items)` counts findings and groups alike, a group by its `count`, into a
`FindingTally`: `{ violation, warning, info, total }`. Both are plain functions, callable from a
Server Component.

## A place, described

```ts
const describe: DescribePlace<Node> = (node) => ({
  where: `${node.path} · ${node.focus}`,
  detail: node.value,
});
```

`where` is what line 1 shows; `detail` is what opening the item shows. A compiler's place is
`{ where: "Line 12" }`. A `FindingPlace` says where a place is, never what to do there: the action is
the item's.

## The item

**`FindingsItem`** draws one finding or a group, from `finding`. Line 1 is the severity — with the
count on the badge, for a group — where, and the item's one `action`; line 2 is the message, on its
own line, and it wraps. Nothing is held to one line but the severity badge and the buttons — a long
IRI breaks rather than widening the list.

- **Where** is one result's place as `describe` reads it, or a group's rule label. Children replace
  it: what a group shares — `totalCost` — is yours to say.
- **`action`** is a `FindingAction`, `{ label, run, pressed? }`: *Go to field*, *Go to line*,
  *Show 362*. One per item, and inside a findings popover the item closes the popover before it runs
  the action, so focus lands where the action sends it. With `pressed` it is Ark's
  [Toggle](/docs/forms/toggle) — *Show* puts something on the page and pressing again takes it off.
- **Opening it** shows one result's `detail`, or a group's sample — each place where it is, its
  message when it differs, and its `detail` — then the rule (label and id) and the help. A sampled
  place is read, not acted on.

`FindingsItem` is a `Diagnostic`, so it goes in a [DiagnosticList](/docs/data-display/diagnostic) or a
`FindingsGroup`, and takes `Diagnostic`'s `open`, `defaultOpen` and `onOpenChange`.

## The words are yours

Nothing tallies in English. The badge's words are its children, a function of the tally, because
*362 violations* and *Valid* are a product's vocabulary and a plural rule is a locale's. The words
the parts draw — the header's title, the severity and the disclosure's name — are `FindingLabels`,
English by default (*Findings*, *Violation*, *Details*), given once on `FindingsRoot` or per item as
`labels`. Counts are formatted in the [LocaleProvider](/docs/i18n)'s locale.

## The badge and its popover

- **`FindingsRoot`** is Ark's [Popover](/docs/overlays/popover), holding the `tally`. `undefined`
  means *nothing checked* — no rules, no run — and is not *clean*. `open`, `defaultOpen` and
  `onOpenChange` are the machine's.
- **`FindingsBadge`** is the trigger: a `Badge` that is a button in every state, named by its
  children. It wears the worst severity found, `success` when the check found nothing and `outline`
  when nothing was checked. `size` and `pill` are `Badge`'s.
- **`FindingsContent`** is the popover, at a fixed width: a `FindingsHeader`, then a `FindingsList`.
- **`FindingsHeader`** is above the list and does not scroll with it. Its title is the labels'
  *Findings*, Ark's popover title; then your parts, in order: **`FindingsSource`**, what was checked —
  `flights-shapes.ttl · 4 rules`, `graph.fossil`, a form's name — muted and monospace;
  **`FindingsActions`**, at the end of the line, for icon buttons (*Check again*, *Stop*, a menu) and
  at most one text toggle; and a [PopoverDescription](/docs/overlays/popover) for what the check
  covered — *1,204 of 3,310 nodes checked*, *2 errors · 1 warning* — on a line of its own.
- **`FindingsList`** is the children in a vertical-only [ScrollArea](/docs/layout/scroll-area), or
  `empty` instead when nothing was found or checked.
- **`FindingsGroup`** puts items under a heading — a `<section>` named by its title — with
  `FindingsTally` beside the title, a count per severity rather than the paths it holds, and one
  `action` at the end of the heading — *Show all 44* — that works as an item's does.
- **`useFindings`** gives a part of your own the tally, the worst severity and the labels.

The props types — `FindingsItemProps`, `FindingsRootProps`, `FindingsBadgeProps`,
`FindingsListProps`, `FindingsGroupProps`, `FindingsTallyProps` — and `FindingAction`,
`FindingPlace`, `FindingRule` are exported for wrappers.
