The graph's settings follow the new Settings panel. The five forces (Gravity, Repulsion, Link spring,
Link distance, Friction) are offered only under Force placement; Clustered offers its Cluster pull,
whose two ends are named *Links decide* and *Tight groups*. Labels comes right after Marks and is a
slider from None to All, since each level adds to the one before. The Map columns read **X axis** and **Y axis**, and the
Clustered column reads **Group by**. Stored values are untouched: a force set under Force is still
applied, and still there when you go back.
