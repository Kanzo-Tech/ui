Ask writes a pattern. An `AskSpec` has `match`, a `Pattern` whose vertices are each named, in place of
`of`, and every field is `variable.property`; a count names a `variable`. An `Answer` and the
`AnswerAdded` *Add to the dashboard* hands you carry `pattern` (and `key`, its `patternKey`) in place
of `relation`. `useAskChat`, `askOffers`, `AnswerCard` and `AskChips` take the `Graph` as `graph`.
A misfit spec is said as `ask/unnamed`, `ask/no-match` or `ask/unmatched`. A chat kept by an earlier
release is refused when it is loaded.
