`Working` takes `value` (`WorkingProps`): a number from 0 to 100 draws `@kanzo-tech/ui`'s `Progress` under the words at that value, `null` draws it indeterminate, and leaving it out draws no bar, as before. A `Progress` you placed beside a `Working` can be passed as its `value` instead. `Tool`'s *Parameters* and *Result* headings are `@kanzo-tech/ui`'s `Eyebrow` and look the same.
