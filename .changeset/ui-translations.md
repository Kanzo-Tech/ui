Every component takes the words it draws through one prop, `translations`, as `LanguagePicker`, `Problem` and `@kanzo-tech/ai` already did. The `Preferences` parts take theirs once on `PreferencesRoot`, which every part inside reads, and a part's own `translations` win for that part; the panel's title, hint, close, Reset and Done, the trigger's name and a sourced choice's *None* are entries now, so a translated panel no longer has English left in it. **Breaking:** pass `translations`, not `copy`, to `PreferencesPanel`, `PreferencesSections` and `Pref`, and the type is `PreferencesTranslations` (was `PreferencesCopy`); `PreferencesPanel`'s `title` and `hint` are entries of its `translations`, and the retired-theme notice's words are `retiredTitle` and `retired(theme)` (were `retired: { title, formatDescription }`). Pass `translations`, not `labels`, to `FindingsRoot`, `FindingsItem`, `FindingsGroup` and `FindingsTally`, and the type is `FindingsTranslations` (was `FindingLabels`). `MadeWith`'s `prefix`, `infix` and `loveLabel` are entries of its `translations`: `<MadeWith translations={{ prefix: "Hecho con", infix: "en", loveLabel: "amor" }}>`.
