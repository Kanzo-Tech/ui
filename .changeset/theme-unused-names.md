Ten names leave `@kanzo-tech/theme`, none of them called by a product: `KanzoThemeName`, which was `string` (write `string`); the section helpers `LookDocument`, `sectionOf`, `withSection`, `fallbackChain` and `resolveSectionToken`; and the density axis's self-check, `checkDensity`, `DENSITY_OBLIGATIONS`, `DensityCheck` and `DensityObligation`. A section's payload is still yours to read and write under `sections[namespace]`, and `validateSection` still checks it against its manifest.
