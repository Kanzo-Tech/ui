`Eyebrow`: the small, muted, uppercase line that names a block from above it — *Parameters*, *Theme*, *How it fits together*. It renders a `<div>`; `asChild` puts the look on the element it heads instead, an `<h3>`, a `FieldLegend`, a `FieldLabel` or a `SliderLabel`. A heading you wrote as `font-medium text-muted-foreground text-xs uppercase tracking-wide` can be replaced with it. `Preferences` draws its headings with it and looks the same; its theme heading is now a `<div>` rather than a `<span>`.
