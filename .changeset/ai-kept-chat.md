A chat can be kept. Give `useAgentChat` or `useAskChat` an `id` with `load(id, { signal })` and `save(id, messages)` — and, if you like, `keep(part, message)` — and the chat of that id is read once before the first turn, validated with the AI SDK's `validateUIMessages` against the `dataPartSchemas` you give the hook, and saved, every message, at the end of every turn, one save at a time. `load` answers `[]` for a chat never saved. `keep` returns the part to store, a rewritten one, or `undefined` to drop it; a message left with no part is not stored, and what it keeps must validate or the save is refused. `chat.kept` says where the chat stands — `loading`, `ready`, `failed` with `chat.kept.error`, or `unsaved` — and `Chat` draws it: its skeleton while the chat is read, and the failure under the transcript through `Problem`, with the composer inert when the chat could not be opened; `translations` gains `unread` and `unsaved` for those. `useAskChat` validates its `answer` and `ask` parts with its own schemas, so you pass it no `dataPartSchemas`; to keep no figure of the data, have `keep` write an answer's `rows` as `[]`. `useKeptChat` does the same for a transcript you draw from messages of your own. If you saved conversations yourself — a store, a queue of saves, a restore — delete that code and hand the hook your `load` and `save`.
